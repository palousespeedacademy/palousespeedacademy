Who We Are

Built for
Competitors

Palouse Speed Academy is a USATF-certified track and field club serving the Palouse region in both Washington and Idaho. We train youth athletes from age 6 through high school, plus open and masters competitors.

Our coaching staff brings professional-level expertise to every session. We don't just run laps — we develop athletes who compete with precision, execute under pressure, and qualify for bigger stages.

Whether you're stepping onto a track for the first time or chasing a regional qualifying mark, PSA is your club.

01

Professional Coaching

USTFCCA certified coaches and former Division I track athletes with degrees in Exercise Science and Education. Proven experience coaching at the youth, high school, and collegiate levels.

02

All Levels Welcome

Ages 6 through adult. From developmental athletes to Junior Olympic qualifiers.

03

Competition Ready

Full USATF Junior Olympics pathway — local meets, Association Championships, Regionals, and Nationals. Top 12 advance from Association to Regionals; top 5 advance to Nationals.

Junior Olympics Pathway

Your Road
to Nationals

1
June 6

Moses Lake All-Comers

Open tune-up meet. Get your first official marks on the board and shake off nerves before the qualifying season begins.

2
June 13

Inland NW Association Championship

Official USATF association meet. Post competitive marks, gain experience in a sanctioned environment before JO qualifying.

3
June 26–27

Inland NW JO Association Championship

The Junior Olympics qualifier. Top 12 finishers per event and age group advance to Region 13 Championships in Tacoma.

4
July 8–12

USATF Region 13 Championship — Tacoma

Regional championship for WA, OR, ID, MT, AK, HI. Top 5 finishers per event advance to the National Junior Olympic Championship.

5
July 27 – August 1

USATF National JO Championship — Norwalk, CA

The national stage. The ceiling of the Junior Olympic pathway. PSA supports all athletes who earn their spot here.
